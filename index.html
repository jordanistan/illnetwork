01 / Get started

A small ritual for a safer machine.

No account, no dashboard maze. Run one command locally and let the scanner show you the parts of your system worth your attention.

Paste this into your terminal to install and start the scanner:

curl -sSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jordanistan/illnetwork/main/setup.sh | bash

When it is ready, open the local address printed in your terminal.
